# Code of conduct

This project is a small, practical one. Everyone who takes part is expected to keep it a place where
people can ask questions, report problems and suggest changes without being treated badly.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to subtitling or to Google Cloud.
- Criticise code, ideas and results, not people.
- Assume good faith, and say plainly when something is unclear instead of guessing.
- Keep private things private: never post another person's keys, addresses, recordings or messages.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Posting someone else's personal information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or repeated unwelcome contact.

## Where it applies

In this repository: issues, pull requests and any other space the project runs.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock conversations, and block people who break these rules.
To report a problem privately, use the
[private report form](https://github.com/muaz978/subtitleedit-gcloud-stt-plugin/security/advisories/new)
and say that it concerns conduct. GitHub's "Report content" option on a comment goes to GitHub, not to the
maintainer.
